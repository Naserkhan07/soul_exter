Scene 1: A lighthouse stands alone on a black rock headland at dusk. Its beam sweeps slowly across heavy grey water. Rain streaks sideways through the light.

Scene 2: Inside the lantern room, brass fittings glow warm against the storm outside. An old keeper winds a mechanism by hand, counting under his breath.

Scene 3: Below in the kitchen, a kettle rattles on a small stove. Charts and tide tables cover the walls, curling at the corners from damp.

Scene 4: The keeper stops and listens. Through the wind comes a faint sound that does not belong to the sea, low and rhythmic and far away.

Scene 5: He climbs the spiral stair two steps at a time, lamp swinging, shadows wheeling around the curved stone wall.

Scene 6: From the gallery he scans the dark water with binoculars. Waves break white over a reef where no vessel should ever be.

Scene 7: A small fishing boat lies pinned against the rocks, its mast broken, a single lantern swinging wildly on the deck.

Scene 8: The keeper hauls the signal lever with both hands. The great beam stops its sweep and holds steady, pointing straight at the wreck.

Scene 9: On the boat, a figure raises an arm into the sudden column of light, waving once, then again.

Scene 10: Dawn. The storm has passed and the sea lies flat and pale. Two figures sit wrapped in blankets on the lighthouse steps, drinking from tin mugs.
