Scene 1: A lone astronaut stands on a ridge of red dune, helmet visor catching a low amber sun. Wind lifts fine dust across the frame in slow ribbons.

Scene 2: Wide shot. The camera pulls back to reveal the wreck of a colony ship half-buried in sand, its hull ribs arching like the bones of a whale.

Scene 3: Inside the wreck. Shafts of dusty light fall through torn plating. The astronaut runs a gloved hand along a frost-covered console and it flickers awake.

Scene 4: Close on the console screen. A looping distress message plays, the face on it grainy and calm, mouthing words we cannot hear.

Scene 5: Outside, a dust storm builds on the horizon, a brown wall swallowing the sky. The astronaut turns to look, small against it.

Scene 6: The astronaut sprints across open ground toward a distant rover, storm light strobing behind, sand whipping sideways.

Scene 7: Interior of the rover. Hands slam the hatch shut. Silence, then the muffled roar of the storm and steady breathing on the mic.

Scene 8: The storm clears at dawn. The rover crests a dune and the camera holds on a green valley below, impossible and bright, under two small moons.
