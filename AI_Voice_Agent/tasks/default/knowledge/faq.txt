Q: Does the AI need a GPU or expensive hardware?
A: No. Our recommended setup uses online speech models, so any laptop works.

Q: Is it expensive to run?
A: You can use free options like Edge voices for speech; the LLM and speech
   models have free tiers. You pay only if you choose a paid provider.

Q: Can it talk in Hindi or Urdu?
A: Yes. It detects the person's language automatically and replies in the
   same language.

Q: Can I change what the AI talks about?
A: Yes, instantly. You edit the task folder — no retraining.

Q: Does it connect to a phone?
A: Yes. It can use your microphone first, and later connect to a phone call
   through an audio bridge.
