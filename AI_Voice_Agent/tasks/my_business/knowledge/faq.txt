Q: What is Polarion?
A: Polarion is a browser-based Application Lifecycle Management (ALM) platform
   by Siemens. It lets engineering teams manage requirements, development,
   testing and release in one connected place, with end-to-end traceability and
   compliance support. It's widely used in automotive, aerospace and medical
   devices.

Q: Can you explain Polarion in detail?
A: Of course. Polarion is an ALM platform. Instead of using separate tools for
   requirements, tests and code, Polarion keeps them linked in one system. When
   a requirement changes, you instantly see which code and tests are affected.
   It supports automated workflows, real-time collaboration, and helps with
   standards like ASPICE, ISO 26262, IEC 62304, DO-178C and FDA/GxP. That's why
   companies in automotive, aerospace and medical devices use it. We implement,
   customise, migrate, train and support Polarion, and we also have our own
   Polarion marketplace apps for reporting and traceability.
