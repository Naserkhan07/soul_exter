POLARION — WHAT WE DO WITH POLARION ALM

WHAT POLARION IS:
Polarion is a browser-based Application Lifecycle Management (ALM) platform
from Siemens. It lets engineering teams manage requirements, development,
testing and release all in one connected environment — instead of using many
separate, disconnected tools. It gives structure, traceability and
auditability across the entire development process.

KEY FEATURES (WHAT WE HELP WITH):
- Requirements management: a central place to write, organise and track
  requirements.
- Test management and defect management.
- End-to-end traceability: link requirements to code and test cases, so when a
  requirement changes you instantly see which code and tests are affected.
- Automated workflows and approvals.
- Reporting & analytics.
- Compliance support: helps with standards like ASPICE, ISO 26262, IEC 62304,
  DO-178C and FDA/GxP, with audit trails and full traceability.
- Real-time collaboration with LiveDocs.

WHO USES IT:
Engineering and software teams in regulated industries — automotive, aerospace,
medical devices and more.

WHAT WE DO WITH POLARION:
- Implementation and setup (install, configure, structure Polarion for your team).
- Customisation (workflows, roles, dashboards, fields tailored to your process).
- Migration (moving from Excel, Jira or other tools into Polarion with clean,
  traceable data).
- Training and ongoing support.

OUR POLARION MARKETPLACE APPS (in the MDIS marketplace):
- Custom ID Script: auto-creates standardized IDs/titles for work items,
  prevents duplicates, improves traceability.
- Customer Form Extension: review and summarize work-item and testing info with
  organized filters and exportable results.
- Workitem History Trend: tracks how work items changed, who changed them and
  when, for configuration management and audits.
- Commenting Report: central view of comments across project documents.
- CR Lifecycle Report: dashboard tracking the status and age of Change Requests.
- Custom H&F Document: standardized headers and footers with branding and
  confidentiality info.
- Baseline Reports: compare two Polarion baselines to show added, removed and
  modified artifacts for change control.
- SDLC Report: centralized view of documentation with eSignature and approval
  status tracking.
- DHF Report: auto-creates a structured Design History File for medical-device
  compliance.
- DD Reports: visualizes parent, derived and branched document relationships.
